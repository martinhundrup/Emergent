THE MODEL / V0.3

What does this model show?

This laboratory combines soft repulsive particles, species-dependent attraction, screened charge, reversible bonds, powered motion, and local catalytic conversions. All units are abstract. The thermal bath and energy source keep the world open to energy flow.

Self-assembly

Size, valence, attraction, and bond turnover determine which structures form and persist. A frozen cluster alone is not evidence of life.

Active matter

Stored energy powers propulsion; local alignment and directional noise can produce collective motion. Turning off the energy source is an informative control experiment.

Autocatalysis

Rules such as feedstock + catalyst → two catalysts can amplify a chemical species. Exhaustion and decay compete with that amplification.

What remains to build

Compartment chemistry, inherited information, resource-coupled reproduction, and selection across generations. This version does not demonstrate open-ended evolution.

Research informing the design

  • Particle Lenia — local interactions and stable patterns ↗

These are inspirations and research directions, not claims that this app reproduces their published results. The repository's RESEARCH.md contains the evidence, limitations, and implementation roadmap.
